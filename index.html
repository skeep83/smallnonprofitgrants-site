Questions people actually ask

How many grants will I actually get each week?

Most members get between 3 and 10. If your niche is narrow, you may get one — or zero some weeks. We would rather send you an empty email than pad it with things that do not fit.

Which countries do you cover?

United States (grants.gov and state funders), the EU Funding & Tenders portal (Horizon, Erasmus+, CERV and friends), and a growing list of national and private funders. Tell us your country and we filter accordingly.

Do you fill out the applications?

No. We find the money and explain it in plain language; the application stays with you, where it belongs. Nobody knows your organization like you do.

What makes this different from saved searches on grant sites?

Saved searches match keywords. We match your mission, your budget size, your legal form and your country — and we read across sources a keyword search cannot cross.

How do I cancel?

Reply STOP to any email. That is all. No call, no retention specialist.

Is this a database I have to log into?

It is an email. Every Tuesday. If you hate databases, you will be fine here.
