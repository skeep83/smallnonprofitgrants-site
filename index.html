Questions people actually ask

How many grants will I actually get each week?

Most readers get between 3 and 10. If your niche is narrow, you may get one — or zero some weeks. We would rather send you an empty email than pad it with things that do not fit.

Which countries do you cover?

United States (grants.gov and state funders), the EU Funding & Tenders portal (CERV, LIFE, Erasmus+, Horizon partnerships), UK government and National Lottery funding, Australia's GrantConnect, plus a growing list of private funders. Tell us your country and we filter accordingly.

Are you a grant database I have to log into?

No. It is an email, every Tuesday. If you hate dashboards, you will be fine here.

Will you spam me the way the big databases do?

One email a week, that is the whole product. Unsubscribe works on the first click — we are not a mailing list with four opt-out pages.

Do you write the applications?

No. We find the money and explain it in plain language; the application stays with you, where it belongs. Nobody knows your organization like you do.

What makes this different from a saved search?

Saved searches match keywords. We match your mission, your budget size, your legal form and your country — across sources a keyword search cannot cross.

How do I cancel?

Reply STOP to any email. That is all. No call, no retention specialist.
