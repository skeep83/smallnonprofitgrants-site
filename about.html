About

A small team, a boring superpower

GrantRadar is run by two people and a very persistent robot. We build automation for a living, and we kept noticing the same thing: small nonprofits were leaving real money on the table — not because they were careless, but because nobody has 40 free hours a week to read government procurement pages.

So we pointed our robot at the feeds. It reads grants.gov, the EU Funding & Tenders portal and a stack of other sources every day of the year, and it has gotten very good at telling the difference between “a grant” and “your grant”.

We are not a marketplace, we take no cut of your funding, and we do not sell your email. If the digest stops being useful, reply STOP and that is the end of it.

Write us — we answer.
