About

Two people and a boring superpower

GrantBrief is run by two people in Europe and a very persistent robot. We build automation for a living, and we kept noticing the same thing: small nonprofits were leaving real money on the table — not because they were careless, but because nobody has 40 free hours a week to read government procurement pages.

So we pointed our robot at the feeds. It reads grants.gov, the EU Funding & Tenders portal, UK and Australian funding services and a stack of foundation feeds every day of the year, and it has gotten good at telling the difference between “a grant” and “your grant”. Two humans check its judgment calls — the shortlist you read is the part a person signed off on.

We are not a marketplace, we take no cut of your funding, and we do not sell your email. If the weekly issue stops being useful, reply STOP and that is the end of it.

Write us — we answer.
